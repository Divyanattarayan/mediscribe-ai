## 1. Important Regulatory & Medical Disclaimer

> **IMPORTANT CLINICAL NOTICE**
> 
> MediScribe AI is an **assistive administrative documentation tool** designed to assist healthcare professionals in transcribing consultations and formatting draft clinical notes.
> 
> - **NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE**: MediScribe AI has not been cleared or approved by the US FDA, EMA, or any national health authority as a medical device or diagnostic clinical decision support system.
> - **NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR CLINICAL JUDGMENT**: The outputs generated by MediScribe AI (including transcribed text, extracted entities, assertions, and draft SOAP notes) must be reviewed, edited, and approved by a licensed healthcare provider before entering any legal or official Electronic Health Record (EHR).
> - **NO DIRECT PATIENT CARE DECISIONS**: The system must never be used autonomously to determine diagnoses, triage urgency, or formulate therapeutic treatment plans without direct physician oversight.

## 2. Model Limitations & Linguistic Boundaries

### 2.1 Code-Switching & Multilingual Acoustic Recognition
- **Current Limitation**: While the underlying Whisper architecture supports multilingual audio, MediScribe AI's primary pipeline and default model (`base.en`) are optimized for English clinical dialogue.
- **Tamil-English & Indic Code-Switching**: Tamil-English mixed consultations ("Tanglish") or other regional code-switching patterns have **not been clinically validated** on this deployment. Clinicians conducting bilingual encounters must expect lower speech recognition accuracy and must perform manual transcript editing.

### 2.2 Assertion & Negation Heuristics
- **Algorithmic Scope**: Clinical assertion detection relies on the `medspaCy` ConText rule-based algorithm and biomedical dictionaries.
- **Failure Modes**:
  - Complex double negatives (e.g., *"not ruling out the absence of..."*) or colloquial sarcasm can lead to misclassification of assertions.
  - Familial history discussions intermingled with patient symptoms without clear pause boundaries may occasionally misattribute an entity experiencer.
- **Mitigation**: All detected entities are presented with assertion tags in the UI for mandatory clinician verification prior to final approval.

### 2.3 LLM Hallucination & Fact Grounding
- **Non-Fabrication Policy**: The system prompt instructs the LLM never to synthesize vital signs, lab values, or examination maneuvers not documented in the dialogue.
- **Evidence Linking**: Every statement in the generated SOAP note is cross-referenced with transcript sentences via `EvidenceLinkingService`. Statements lacking transcript alignment are explicitly flagged with `"Evidence not found — review required."`

## 3. Data Privacy & Audio Retention Policy

### 3.1 Audio File Lifecycle
1. **Monolithic Pipeline (`/api/transcribe`)**:
   - Audio files uploaded through this route are stored temporarily in a restricted directory.
   - Processing is wrapped in a `try...finally` block that deletes the raw audio from disk immediately after transcription and extraction complete.
2. **Stepwise Consultation Pipeline (`/api/consultations/upload`)**:
   - Audio files are stored in `uploads/consultations/{consultation_id}/` to allow playback during clinician transcript review.
   - **Explicit Deletion**: Clinicians can delete audio files at any time via `DELETE /api/consultations/{id}/audio` while preserving the approved textual note.
   - **Retention Expiry**: System administrators can configure automated retention scripts (e.g., 24-hour retention) to purge raw audio after final note sign-off.

### 3.2 Sensitive Data & Logging Boundaries
- **No Transcript Logging**: Raw transcripts, patient identifiers, and clinical notes are never written to unencrypted application console logs or standard log files.
- **No Credential Logging**: Authorization headers, passwords, and API keys are strictly excluded from debug logs.
- **Database Isolation**: All consultation records are isolated by tenant (`user_id`). Clinicians can only query, edit, or delete their own consultation records.

### 3.3 Synthetic Data for Demonstration & Development
- In development and demonstration modes, synthetic patient consultation recordings and mock transcripts are utilized to demonstrate functionality without handling real Protected Health Information (PHI).
- Any real patient audio recorded during clinical pilots must be captured only after obtaining explicit patient informed consent pursuant to local health data privacy regulations (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR).

## 4. API Key & Secrets Handling

- **Zero Client Exposure**: Third-party API keys (including `GROQ_API_KEY`) reside exclusively in backend environment variables and are never transmitted to the browser or embedded in client bundles.
- **Secret Separation**: Sensitive secrets are stored in `.env` (excluded from version control via `.gitignore`). Production deployments must inject secrets via encrypted container environment configurations.
